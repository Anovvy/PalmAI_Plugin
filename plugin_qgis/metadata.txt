about=This plugin has 4 tools, namely Palm Oil Tree Counting, Palm Oil Tree Classification, Palm Oil Centre Tree, and road detection. Palm Oil Tree Counting is used for tree counting of oil palm trees, Palm Oil Tree Classification is used to classify oil palm trees based on their appearance from aerial photographs. Palm Oil Center Tree is used for precision mapping, namely the placement of points in the middle of the crown of oil palm trees, and Road detection is used to extract roads from aerial photographs of oil palm plantation areas and convert them into vector files. The output of this plugin is in the form of bounding boxes, points, and line in shapefile(.shp) format.
